Nairobi's property market rewards enthusiasm and punishes shortcuts. The gap between a dream purchase and a courtroom dispute is almost always diligence that never happened. Run these checks before any deposit moves.

## The ten essentials

1. **Official registry search** — confirms the registered owner and any encumbrances on the title.
2. **Encumbrance tracing** — charges, caveats and restrictions must be discharged, not merely acknowledged.
3. **Rates clearance** — unpaid county rates transfer into disputes; obtain the clearance certificate.
4. **Rent clearance** — for leasehold land, confirm no rent arrears with the Commissioner of Lands.
5. **Survey verification** — the beacons on the ground must match the registry index map.
6. **Company search** — where the seller is a company, verify the signatory's authority to sell.
7. **Probate confirmation** — where the seller is an estate administrator, the confirmed grant must cover this parcel.
8. **Spousal consent** — matrimonial property requires the spouse's documented consent to the sale.
9. **Land control consent** — agricultural land outside municipalities needs the board's consent within the statutory window.
10. **Approvals check** — for developments, county approvals and occupation certificates confirm what you are buying is legal.

## The pattern behind the pain

Almost every failed transaction we rescue skipped items in the first five. Fraudsters exploit urgency: any pressure to deposit "before someone else sees it" is itself a warning sign.
